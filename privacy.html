Privacy Policy

Operated by Adam Sangary ("Qur'anic Academy Center", "QAC", "we", "us"). QAC is held personally by Adam Sangary and is not part of Sangary Enterprise LLC. Effective date: the launch date of the QAC mobile applications.

Qur'anic Academy Center — "Where children grow with the Qur'an and the Sunnah" — is an online school where children learn the Qur'an, Hadith and Arabic through recorded lessons and live video classes with verified teachers. The service is provided through our website (quranacademycenter.com), our installable web app, and our mobile applications (together, the "Service").

QAC is a school for children, so this policy is written with children's privacy at its center. Accounts are held by parents and guardians ("parents"). Children use the Service only under a parent's account, with the parent's consent.

1. Information we collect

Parent account information. When a parent creates an account we collect the parent's name, email address and/or phone number, password or verification code, language preference, and time zone (so class times are shown correctly in the family's local time). If the parent later subscribes to a paid plan, payment is handled by our payment processor — we receive confirmation of payment, the amount, plan and a transaction reference. We do not store full card numbers.

Child profile information. Under their own account, a parent creates a profile for each child containing only: the child's first name, age or level, language, and — if the parent chooses — a profile photo. The parent can add, replace or remove the photo at any time. Children do not have their own email addresses or passwords; a child signs in with their profile and a picture code under the parent's account. We do not ask children for their full name, home address, school name, or location.

Learning and progress information. Lessons watched and completed, surahs and verses memorized, practice time and repetitions, milestones and level progress, teacher progress notes, and assignments.

Practice recordings. A child may optionally record their recitation of assigned verses inside the Service. These recordings are sent only to that child's own teacher, who uses them to correct the recitation at the next live class. They are never public and are never visible to other students.

Live class recordings. Live classes are recorded by default for child safety (see Section 4). Recordings are stored under our control and are accessible only to the child's parent (for their own child's class), the class teacher, and authorized administrators for safeguarding review.

Teacher information. Teachers provide government-issued photo ID, qualification documents (such as ijazah certificates or degrees), a live selfie for identity matching, and background-screening information where obtainable, as described in our safeguarding standard. This information is used only for verification and safeguarding and is never shown to students; a teacher's profile photo and first name are shown to their own students and those students' parents.

Showcase posts. A child's photo or recitation video appears in the public Showcase only when the parent has given explicit consent for that specific post. A Showcase post shows the child's first name, level and achievement only — never a surname, location or school. Consent is per-post and can be withdrawn at any time, which removes the post.

Device and usage information. Device type, operating system, app version, and how the Service is used, used to keep the Service working, to provide offline access to lessons and the Qur'an, and to fix problems. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles of children (see Section 3).

Support communications. Messages a parent sends us through the Service's chat or by email, and our replies.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: create and manage accounts, place children in levels and sections, schedule and run live classes, deliver lessons and practice tools, and track progress.
  • To keep children safe: verify teachers, record live classes, monitor classes for safeguarding, investigate reports, and enforce our safeguarding policy.
  • To communicate with parents: class reminders, progress reports, account and subscription notices, and support replies. We do not send marketing messages to children.
  • To operate and improve the school: understand how lessons are used in aggregate, fix problems, and develop the curriculum.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Children's privacy — our commitments

  • Parental consent first. A child's profile is created only by their parent or guardian, who consents to the collection described in this policy when they sign up. This is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR's protections for children's data (GDPR-K).
  • No advertising to children. The Service shows no third-party advertisements to children, and no child's data is used for advertising.
  • No sale of data. We do not sell personal information — children's or parents' — to anyone.
  • No behavioral tracking of children. We do not allow third-party trackers to profile children across apps or websites, and we do not share children's data with data brokers or advertising networks.
  • Data minimization. A child profile holds the minimum the school needs: first name, age/level, language, progress, and an optional parent-controlled photo.
  • No private contact. There is no private messaging between teachers and students, or between students, anywhere in the Service. All communication happens inside the live class, with the teacher present, or between the parent and QAC.
  • Parent control. A parent can review their child's information, correct it, withdraw Showcase consent, and request export or deletion of their child's data at any time (see Section 6).

4. Live class recording notice

Live classes are recorded by default. Recording is a safeguarding measure: it means no adult is ever unobserved with children on QAC, and any concern can be reviewed on evidence. By enrolling a child in live classes, the parent acknowledges and consents to this recording. Recordings are stored securely under administrator control, are never published or shared publicly, and are made available to the parent of a child in the class on request. Parents may also observe their own child's live class at any time through the Parent Section.

5. How information is shared

  • Within the school, on a need-to-see basis. A child's own teacher sees the child's first name, photo (if the parent provided one), level and progress for their section only. A teacher never sees another teacher's sections or students. Administrators can access records for operations and safeguarding.
  • With service providers who help us run the Service (such as video-classroom, hosting, and payment providers), only the information needed for their work, under agreements that require them to protect it. Live classes run on an established video provider integrated into the platform; recordings remain under our access rules.
  • For legal and safety reasons, if required by law or court order, or to protect a child's safety.
  • Showcase, only as described in Section 1 — per-post parental consent, first name and level only, removable on withdrawal.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share children's information with advertisers.

6. Retention, export and deletion

  • Account, profile and progress information is kept while the account is active, so a child's learning record is continuous.
  • Live class recordings are kept for a limited safeguarding retention period and then deleted, unless a recording must be kept longer to resolve a safeguarding report or meet a legal duty.
  • Practice recordings are kept while the child is enrolled with that teacher and are deleted on the parent's request.
  • A parent may at any time request a copy (export) of their child's data, or deletion of their child's profile and associated data. Deletion requests are honored except where a record must be kept to resolve an open safeguarding matter or meet a legal obligation; we will tell the parent if that applies. Contact us using Section 9.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, access controls that limit each teacher and administrator to only the records their role requires, and session recording and admin monitoring of live classes. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Age and accounts

Accounts are held only by parents, guardians and verified teachers — adults. Children never hold accounts, payment rights or contact rights of their own; they access the Service through a child profile under a parent's account, using the simple picture-code sign-in. The Service is designed for children ages 3–18 learning under their parent's supervision.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes — especially anything affecting children's data — will be announced to parents in the Service and/or by email before they take effect, and continued use after the effective date will be treated as acceptance by the account-holding parent. The version in force at launch carries the launch date as its effective date.
